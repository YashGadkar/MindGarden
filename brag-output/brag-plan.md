# Brag Plan: MindGarden

## What is this app?
MindGarden is an intelligent, multi-stakeholder collegiate mental health prediction and support ecosystem developed as a Community Engineering Project (CEP), combining 18-feature XGBoost gradient-boosted trees with the human-centered "Ditto" design system to safeguard campus wellbeing before crises emerge.

## The angle
Traditional campus mental health fails because it is entirely passive: universities wait for students in distress to hit a crisis point before offering help, while academic observations and clinical care operate in disconnected silos. MindGarden reverses this dynamic into proactive early intervention: daily student habit check-ins and faculty mentorship observations feed into an XGBoost inference pipeline, giving campus counsellors a real-time clinical priority queue to reach students before they burn out.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A stark, high-contrast typographic problem statement: "Campus mental health shouldn't wait for a crisis." It confronts the passive legacy status quo, then blooms into the warm, empathetic palette of MindGarden: "Introducing a proactive intervention ecosystem."

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The Tactile Daily Check-in:** A student sliding habit sliders (sleep, study hours, screen time, financial stress) with the Ditto design system's sunlit wildflower warmth and responsive micro-feedback.
- **The XGBoost Intelligence Engine:** Real-time multi-class risk scoring on an 18-feature psychological and academic vector, mapping vulnerability trajectory.
- **The Stakeholder Triad & Counsellor Queue:** A unified clinical triage queue that alerts campus counsellors with high-priority risk indicators, bridging students, faculty mentors, and clinicians without privacy friction.

## Outro / punchline
"Early prediction. Empathetic intervention. Zero stigma."
The blooming MindGarden mark settles with clear collegiate project credentials: "Community Engineering Project (CEP) · Built with Flask, XGBoost & Ditto Design System."

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry:** Student completes the daily habit & wellness check-in with tactile range sliders.
2. **Inference:** XGBoost evaluates the 18-feature vector and computes the multi-class risk index (Low / Medium / High).
3. **Action:** Counsellor Priority Queue surfaces high-vulnerability students for immediate, confidential clinical outreach.
