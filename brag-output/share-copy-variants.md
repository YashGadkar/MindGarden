## LinkedIn (Community & Engineering Focus)
Collegiate mental health systems have traditionally operated on passive, delayed models — intervening only after students reach an acute crisis point.

As our Community Engineering Project (CEP), we built **MindGarden**: a multi-stakeholder proactive intervention ecosystem designed to safeguard campus wellbeing.

Key Architecture Highlights:
- **Predictive Intelligence:** Multi-class XGBoost classifier analyzing an 18-feature psychological and academic vector in real time.
- **The Stakeholder Triad:** Bridges student daily habit check-ins, faculty mentorship observations, and campus counsellors without privacy friction.
- **Human-Centered UI:** Designed with the "Ditto" design system (warm canvas, tactile sliders, accessible contrast) to eliminate clinical intimidation.

Built with Python, Flask, XGBoost, and modern web standards.
